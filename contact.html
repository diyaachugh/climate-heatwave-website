Useful References

India Meteorological Department (IMD)

India's national weather agency, responsible for official forecasts and heat alerts.

World Health Organization (WHO)

Publishes global guidance on the health risks of extreme heat and how to reduce them.

National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA)

Coordinates India's disaster preparedness and response, including heat action plans.

NASA Earth Observatory

Provides satellite imagery and climate data used to track large-scale heat patterns.

Project Goal

This website demonstrates how Climate Intelligence can help monitor weather conditions, predict future heatwaves, and issue early warnings that reduce health risks and improve disaster preparedness.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this connected to real weather data?

No — this is a concept and design project. The sample readouts and figures shown are for illustration only, not live sensor data.

What would a real version of this need?

A live version would need a data pipeline from weather stations and satellites, a trained prediction model, and an alert system connected to local authorities.

Why focus on heatwaves specifically?

Heatwaves are becoming more frequent with climate change, yet they're often under-warned compared to other disasters like floods or cyclones, making early warning systems especially valuable.
