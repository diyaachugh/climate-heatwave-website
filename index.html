Project Overview

What it does

Bringing three things together

Climate Intelligence brings together weather monitoring, artificial intelligence, and forecasting techniques into a single pipeline: watch current conditions, predict heatwaves ahead of time, and turn that prediction into a warning people can actually act on. The goal is to shrink the gap between the heat arriving and the community knowing to prepare for it.

Why this matters: heatwaves are becoming more frequent and more intense as the climate changes, but most heat-related harm is preventable if people get a clear warning early enough to act on it.
Objectives

What it aims to achieve

  • Monitor temperature and related conditions continuously
  • Predict heatwaves using AI-driven forecasting
  • Generate early warnings before extreme heat arrives
  • Improve public safety and disaster preparedness

How It Works

Step 1

Monitor

Continuously track temperature, humidity, wind and solar radiation from weather stations and satellites.

Step 2

Predict

Feed that data into a machine learning model trained to recognise the patterns that precede a heatwave.

Step 3

Warn

Turn the prediction into a clear, timely alert so people can prepare before conditions turn severe.
